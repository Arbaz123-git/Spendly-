Privacy Policy

Learn how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate Spendly: to let you record expenses, view spending summaries, and filter by time period. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained only as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated expense data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team through the details provided on the site.
